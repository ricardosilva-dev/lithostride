## 17_terra_mineral_variacoes

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ORIGINAL pixel art game assets for LITHOSTRIDE, a 2D side-view mining survival game on a living mineral colossus. References are style/palette examples only. Ground assets must harmonize with mossy olive grass, warm earthen brown dirt and slate rock. Deliberate crisp pixel clusters, restrained small palette, no photorealistic/painterly texture, no soft gradients, no 3D render. This is source art for later exact slicing and edge normalization, no text, numbers, labels, watermark or decorative frame. A TERRAIN INTERIOR TEXTURE ATLAS: 6 columns x 4 rows of 24 distinct SQUARE fully filled warm brown dirt texture samples, matching the reference dirt but MUCH LOWER CONTRAST and without the repetitive pebble wallpaper. Each square is front-on flat opaque material with no object depth, bevel, rim, shadow outline, grassy top or soil island. Small transparent even gutters separate the samples. Row 1 fine compact earth with very sparse microgranules; row 2 compact soil with subtle irregular crumb clusters; row 3 a few small embedded flat stones, never one central rock; row 4 slight clay-rich variations and scattered tiny gravel. All variants share the SAME warm midbrown edge palette and balanced brightness so they can become connected terrain. Distribution deliberately irregular and non-directional, no repeated diamonds, no vertical columns, no tiled decorative pattern. Prefer about 16x16 logical-pixel texture vocabulary per sample enlarged with nearest-neighbor look; few readable pixel clusters not busy noise. Wide 1536x1024 sheet, all samples same size. Real transparent alpha gutters only; every material square completely opaque.
